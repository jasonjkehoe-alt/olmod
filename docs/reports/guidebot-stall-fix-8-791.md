## The fix (OLMod commit a2403b2, on 82f5197, branch guidebot-stuck-return)

### P0 — far-centre cycle-break (GuidebotSteering.cs, HoldPortalLine)

- Header comment updated (lines 1373–1390) to record the Nov 23 stall as the case that generalised Case C.
- Old Case C (nearDoorway-gated, 3.0 m clamp to portal centre / doorway crossing) REPLACED by the generalised clamp (lines 1519–1580): when the bot is in the traversing leg's fromSeg and its current destination is the centre of a route segment at pos+2 or beyond (a visibility advance the bot has not earned by crossing the current leg's portal), redirect the destination to the current leg's portal centre pushed 1.5 m beyond the plane along the portal normal (the push-through point Cases A/B already use). For doorway legs the target is dw.Crossing + portalNormal × 1.5 (the crossing is the validated point; the portal centre may be buried/jamb-blocked); for non-doorway legs it is pc + portalNormal × 1.5; fallback to pc/crossing when the normal can't be read. The clamp does NOT touch the portal flag (the stock branch re-sets it; Fix A + the portal-aware pull-back handle the index). It does NOT fire when the dest is already the portal centre, the next (pos+1) centre, or a staged crossing/approach from the movement prefix.
- Why it breaks the cycle: with the flag true, the stock portal branch re-aims at Global_path[Global_path_index] and the postfix pins the index to pos+1=791, so the re-aim is forward; the clamp only fires on far-centre frames, and on portal-centre frames the bot flies toward the portal centre — the two aims (portal centre vs push-through point) are nearly collinear from 13.4 m, so AddForce builds in one direction.
- Benign-leg note: the dest DOES flip to a pos+2 centre on healthy legs too (e.g. in seg 213 the dest alternated between 850's centre (pos+1) and 849's centre (pos+2)). On those frames the clamp redirects to the 213→850 portal push-through point (390.63, 54.81, 460.59) — a forward aim ~12° off the 850-centre aim, so it cannot stall the leg. The replay (§6) proves the clamp does NOT fire on the 213 or 827 approach dests.

### P1 — free-space stall sensor (GuidebotStuckReturn.cs)

- Constants (lines 113–122): kFreeStallSpeed 0.05, kFreeStallDestDist 1.0, kFreeStallMove 0.5 m, kFreeStallWindow 12.0 s, kForcefieldWaitRadius 6.0 m.
- CheckFreeSpaceStall (lines 344–441), called from the PrefixInner (line 199): a second, independent stuck sensor keyed off the stuck-watch condition — |v| < 0.05 AND destination > 1.0 m away AND the bot's position unchanged (under 0.5 m movement from the window-open position) for a continuous 12 s window. The window opens when the still condition first holds, resets if the condition breaks, if the bot moves ≥0.5 m, if the submode leaves the goal set, or if the game clock goes backward (level restart). It feeds the EXISTING recovery path (return-to-player if the player is behind on the route, teleport-forward if ahead; limitless; logged). Self-consuming on fire (re-arm for a 12 s retry cadence if the recovery declines). IsGoalSubmode (line 310) shares the exclusion list (GO_TO_PLAYER, WANDER_NEAR_PLAYER, NONE, GO_AWAY, NAPPING, START) between the sensor and the recovery; gameTime was moved up in PrefixInner and the duplicate declaration removed. Velocity is read via the existing s_rigidbodyField; if the rigidbody is missing, velocity reads 0 but the position check still guards (a moving bot's position changes >0.5 m).
- **Forcefield-wait handling (two independent guards):** (a) IsWaitingAtActiveForcefield (line 443): `UnityEngine.Object.FindObjectsOfType<Forcefield>()` + `gameObject.activeSelf` check, radius 6.0 m from the bot. Rationale from the decompile (Forcefield: PropBase, m_disabled private, Deactivate() sets m_disabled=true + c_go.SetActive(false); the game itself uses FindObjectsOfType in DeactivateAllForcefields / UpdateSegmentsForForcefields): any Forcefield found is UP, since deactivation makes the GameObject inactive and FindObjectsOfType returns active objects only (Unity 2017). A bot pressing an UP field sits ~1–2 m from the field prop (it aims at the portal centre where the prop sits); 6 m is generous; the 8→791 stall sat 23+ m from the field. Called only when the sensor window completes (≤ once per 12 s), never every frame. Suppression re-arms the window (re-checks after another 12 s) and logs once per suppression: `[GuidebotStuckReturn] free-space sensor suppressed: bot still 12 s but an active forcefield is near (waiting for the field, not wedged); re-arming`. (b) The existing player-position rule: teleport-forward only fires when the player is AHEAD on the route (already past the field); a return only brings the bot BACK to the player. Neither guard alone would be enough; together the sensor cannot teleport the bot past an active forcefield.
- Log line on fire: `[GuidebotStuckReturn] free-space stall: bot still 12 s, |v|=...` (then the existing recovery log lines).
